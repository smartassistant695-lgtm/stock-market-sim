How it works

  • You will trade one practice stock and then four stocks. The companies are made up. Their price movements come from real stock market data.
  • For each stock you start with $10,000 of virtual money and no shares.
  • The chart shows one day of price history. Then the price starts moving.
  • The chart stops 4 times for each of the four stocks (2 times in the practice). Each time, enter a number of shares on the keypad and tap Buy or Sell, or tap Hold to do nothing.
  • Optional: set a stop loss or take profit to sell automatically if the price reaches a level you choose.
  • After each stock you will answer one question about it.
  • Try to end with as much money as you can.
  • Tap Directions at any time to see these steps again.

Reading the chart

Each candle shows the price over a period of time: the top of the wick is the highest price and the bottom is the lowest. Green means the price ended higher than it started, and red means it ended lower. The label in the top corner of the chart shows how much time each candle covers.
